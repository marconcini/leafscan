🌿 LeafScan

Plant disease from a leaf photo — runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

Loading the model (≈ 32 MB, once)…

What this is. A ConvNeXt-T trained on lab photos (PlantVillage), then fine-tuned on web and field photos (PlantDoc), for 38 crop / disease classes of 14 crops. This page runs an int8-quantised copy of the model with ONNX Runtime Web.

How much to trust it. On held-out field photos it is right about 72% of the time. Below a confidence threshold fitted on field photos it says "Not sure": it answers about two thirds of field photos, and about 85% of those answers are right. It only knows these 38 classes and is not a substitute for an agronomist.

Code, experiments and limitations: github.com/marconcini/leafscan · model: Marconcini/leafscan-convnext-tiny
